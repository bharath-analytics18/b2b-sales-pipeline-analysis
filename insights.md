1. Win rate is steady, so revenue differences come from deal size and volume.
The overall win rate is 63.15%, and all six managers sit between 62.1% and 64.4%. Teams don’t differ much in how often they win. They differ in how much each win is worth and how many deals they close.
Recommendation: focus coaching on deal size and deal volume, not on closing skills.

2. Revenue depends heavily on one agent.
Darcel Schlecht brought in 1,153,214, about 11.5% of all revenue, and about 51% of Melvin Marxen’s team total. The second-best agent made 478,396.
Recommendation: spread his methods and accounts across the team, and watch the risk if he leaves.

3. Three products drive most of the revenue.
GTX Pro, GTX Plus Pro and MG Advanced bring in about 8.36M, or roughly 84% of revenue. MG Special wins 793 deals but earns only about 44K, because each deal is worth about 55.
Recommendation: prioritise the three top products, and review whether MG Special deals are worth the sales effort.

4. Revenue fell from Q2 to Q4.
Quarterly revenue dropped from about 3.09M in Q2 to 2.80M in Q4, down about 9%. Over the same period, won deals fell about 5% and average deal size fell about 5%. Q1 is left out because it only contains March.
Recommendation: investigate whether the product mix or deal quality changed. The data shows that revenue fell but not why.
